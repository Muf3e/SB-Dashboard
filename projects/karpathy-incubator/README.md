## Executive Overview
The Karpathy LLM Council eliminates executive echo chambers and 'yes-men' thinking by subjecting every new venture, product, and strategic pivot to rigorous, adversarial first-principles stress-testing.

## Three-Stage Deliberation Protocol
1. **Round 1 (Flaw Interrogation & Alternate Options)**:
   - Council debators identify critical operational, financial, and market risks.
   - Crucially, every criticism must supply an actionable alternate option or pivot.
2. **Round 2 (Cross-Examination & Stress-Testing)**:
   - Debators challenge proposed alternatives and test extreme edge cases.
3. **Round 3 (Consensus Convergence & Veto Withdrawal)**:
   - Debators resolve trade-offs, drop vetoes once mitigations are locked, and ratify a final execution blueprint.

## Debator Personas
- **The Contrarian**: Devil's advocate and systemic vulnerability scanner.
- **The First Principles Thinker**: Physics and fundamental economic constraint auditor.
- **The Executor**: Monday-morning operational planner.
