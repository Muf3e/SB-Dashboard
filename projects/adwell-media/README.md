## Executive Overview
AdWell Media disrupts physical out-of-home advertising by turning 500ml spring water bottles into high-engagement mini billboards. Premium brand sponsors cover the entire production and distribution cost, allowing consumers to receive free drinking water while providing sponsors with 100% captive attention.

## The Model
1. **Free Water for Consumers**: Distributed at captive high-dwell locations (diagnostic clinics, luxury wedding receptions, executive summits).
2. **High-ROI Sponsor Billboard**: Full-wrap, water-resistant labels featuring high-impact branding and dedicated scannable QR codes.
3. **QR Attention Analytics**: Every bottle scan routes through SB Group tracking endpoints, providing sponsors with verified engagement attribution.
